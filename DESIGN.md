# Design System: Keep

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Calm Ocean Workspace"**

Keep is an Operate surface: operators work it at desk monitors through a shift, and dashboards also run fullscreen on NOC wall displays read from across the room. The system as built is the incumbent Keep workspace (structure of commit `f58c7d0`) recoloured with the user-chosen Ocean palette on 2026-10-26. Nothing moved: the same 76px icon rail, crumb-and-title page header, CEL bar, quick views, white table panels, Geist and Geist Mono, 8px controls and 12px panels. What changed is temperature. The shell reads as a cool blue tint instead of warm grey, and the blue marks exactly two things: where to act and where you are.

Density is moderate and even. Body text is 13.5px, rows are 48–52px, panels are flat white cards with a hairline border on a faintly blue canvas. Colour is semantic, not decorative: every colour in the feature styles resolves to one role in `src/app/theme.css`, defined once for light (the default) and once for dark. Status and severity keep their red, orange, amber and green meanings, tuned to sit inside the palette, and every severity and status colour sits beside its text label. Dense Feed and preset rows show the coloured label alone; detail headers and other lists add the shape-coded mark. Since REV-43 the header shows update status only when something is wrong, and no revision numbers appear on the alert, preset or dashboard screens.

The confirmed rejections come from the user: no black-and-white or grayscale palette, no black primary buttons, no generic SaaS admin look, no neon hacker console, no playful consumer styling, and nothing structural from the Ocean prototype (no 228px labelled sidebar, no Mulish or IBM Plex Mono, no halo on firing dots, none of its spacing or size changes).

**Key Characteristics:**
- Incumbent structure, Ocean colour: cool blue-tinted rail and canvas, white panels, navy ink.
- One solid blue for primary actions, Run, the active rail tile and the selected tab underline; a soft blue pill for the selected quick view. The Lighthouse brand mark has its own four roles.
- Semantic roles only, light and dark, each text role at 4.5:1 or better on its surfaces.
- Flat bordered panels at rest; navy-tinted shadows only on things that float.
- Geist for interface text, including times relative to now; Geist Mono for CEL, exact timestamps, source lines and board counts.

## Colors

A cool, restrained Ocean palette: blue-tinted neutrals carry the screen, one saturated blue carries action and location, and warm status hues appear only where they mean something.

### Severity and Feedback
- **Severity text / mark pairs**: Critical `critical` #b42318 / `critical-mark` #d92d20 (dark #ff958c / #f0645a); High `high` #b4480c / `high-mark` #ec6a13 (dark #f6a774 / #f08a4b); Warning and Medium `amber` #8a6100 / `amber-mark` #d99a06 (dark #ecc76a / #e5b53d). Low and Clear use `muted` text with a `faint` ring. The text colour is tuned for 4.5:1; the mark colour is tuned for presence.
- **OK** (`ok` #2e9e62 mark, `ok-ink` #1d6e45 text, `ok-soft`/`ok-soft-2` grounds, `ok-line`): Resolved status and its quick-view dot, success banners, string literals in CEL.
- **Warning** (`warning` #7a5600 text on `warning-bg`/`warning-soft`, `warning-line`, `warning-hover`): notices and unsaved-change chips. The update notice's dot is `amber-mark`.
- **Error** (`error` #a01d13 on `error-bg` #fdeeec with `error-line`): error banners and inline errors.
- **Danger** (`danger` #b42318 / #b4413b with white `danger-text`, `danger-hover`): solid destructive confirmation buttons only.

### Named Rules
**The One Blue Rule.** Solid Ocean blue means "act here" or "you are here": primary buttons, Run, the active rail tile, the selected tab underline and focus. A selected filter is the soft `primary-soft` pill, never the solid fill, and blue is never used as decoration.

**The Roles Only Rule.** Every colour in feature styles is a `var(--role)` from `src/app/theme.css`, and each role is defined exactly twice, once for light and once for dark. No raw hex or rgba appears outside the token layer; shadows are built from `--shadow-rgb` and `--shade-*`.

**The 4.5 Floor Rule.** Every text role meets 4.5:1 on the surfaces it is used on, in both themes, placeholders included (`subtle` is 5.65:1 on white and 6.66:1 on dark panels). The Run button's Ctrl+Enter hint renders at 0.9 opacity and still measures 4.67:1 in light and 6.99:1 in dark.

## Typography

**Body Font:** Geist (local variable font, weights 100–900; fallback system-ui, sans-serif)
**Label/Mono Font:** Geist Mono (local variable font; fallback ui-monospace, monospace)

**Character:** One neutral grotesque for the whole interface, with its monospace sibling for anything a machine wrote or a person types as a query. Hierarchy comes from size and weight steps of 500 and 600, not from a second display face.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (Geist Mono 600, 56px or 36px, line-height 1, -0.02em, tabular): dashboard count values only, sized for wall displays.
- **Headline** (Geist 600, 24px, line-height 1.3, -0.01em): the page title beside its subtitle; 21px at 639px and below; 20px on the sign-in card; 22px in fullscreen board bars.
- **Title** (Geist 600, 15px): panel headings; 16px in dialogs; 14px for alert messages in rows.
- **Body** (Geist 400, 13.5px, line-height 1.5): default text. Controls, table cells and tabs run at 13px 500.
- **Label** (Geist 600, 12px): field labels and severity text at 12.5px; 11.5px for help text, chips and captions; 10.5px 500 for rail labels.
- **Mono** (Geist Mono, 12.5px on a 21px line in CEL fields; 11.5px for source sub-lines, timeline meta and filter chips; 12px for inline code).

### Named Rules
**The Mono-for-Machine Rule.** Geist Mono is reserved for CEL, exact timestamps and source sub-lines, filter chips and board counts. Everything a person reads as interface copy is Geist, including times relative to now (“30 seconds ago”), whose exact timestamp stays in the tooltip. Counts and paging numbers use tabular figures.

## Layout

A fixed 76px icon rail sits at the left edge of every signed-in screen; the page fills the rest at full viewport height, with the table panel growing to fill the remaining space and scrolling internally. The page header is padded 16px 24px 14px and holds a crumb row (tenant selector, area, access chip) above the title row, with the live pill and page actions right-aligned. The page body uses 24px side gutters, 20px bottom padding and a 12px vertical gap between the CEL bar, quick views and panel. Panel headers pad 13px 18px, panel bodies 16px 18px, rows 0 18px.

Spacing steps observed in reuse are 4, 6, 8, 12, 14, 16, 18 and 24px. Responsive changes: at 1150px side columns narrow (detail 320px, editor 280px); at 1023px the page height becomes auto, the header and actions wrap and grid tables scroll horizontally at fixed minimum widths (1180px full alert grid, 720px narrow); at 900px editors and detail panels stack and dashboard panels go two-up; at 639px the rail narrows to 60px, gutters drop to 16px and the title to 21px. Fullscreen dashboards use the canvas colour edge to edge with 20px 24px padding.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat by default. Panels, tables, inputs and cards sit on the canvas with a 1px border and no shadow (`--shadow: none`); depth comes from the white-on-tinted-canvas step and from borders. Shadows appear only on objects that float above the page and on a few state cues. In light they are navy-tinted (rgb 11 37 69); in dark they are black at higher alpha.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Selected segment** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgb(var(--shadow-rgb) / 0.12)`): the raised thumb of a segmented control.
- **Panel hover** (`box-shadow: 0 6px 18px rgb(var(--shadow-rgb) / 0.08)`): dashboard count panels on hover.
- **Popover** (`box-shadow: 0 8px 24px var(--shade-3)`): CEL completion list and menus; the account menu uses `0 8px 32px var(--shade-3)`.
- **Bulk bar** (`box-shadow: 0 12px 32px rgb(var(--shadow-rgb) / 0.28)`): the floating selection bar.
- **Dialog** (`box-shadow: 0 16px 40px rgb(var(--shadow-rgb) / 0.12)`) over a scrim of `rgb(var(--shadow-rgb) / 0.28)`.
- **Sign-in card** (`box-shadow: 0 12px 40px rgb(var(--shadow-rgb) / 0.06)`).

### Named Rules
**The Flat-at-Rest Rule.** Resting surfaces never carry a shadow. A shadow means the object floats (popover, menu, bulk bar, dialog) or is the selected thumb of a control.

## Shapes

Gently rounded, nested by size: chips and small inline buttons at 6px, controls (buttons, inputs, quick views, code fields) at 8px, the segmented track at 9px, banners and rail tiles at 10px, panels and tables at 12px, dialogs at 14px, and the sign-in card at 16px. The live pill, theme switch and status pills are fully rounded. Borders are always 1px hairlines, except the 2px selected-tab underline, 2px focus outlines and the threshold accent on dashboard count panels. Status dots are 7–8px circles; severity marks are shape-coded (10px rounded square for Critical, 8px diamond for High, 6px rings for Warning and Low).

### Theme switch and theme mechanism
A sun/moon switch in the account menu: a 56×30 `fill-strong` track with a 24px `surface` thumb that slides 26px in 160ms (no motion under reduced-motion). Light is the default regardless of OS preference; a dark choice is stored as `keep-theme` in localStorage and applied as `data-theme` on `<html>` by an inline bootstrap script before first paint. Each theme sets `color-scheme`, so native controls, scrollbars and form widgets follow. Browser surfaces are themed from the palette: `accent-color` and `caret-color` are `primary`, `::selection` is `primary-soft` with `text`, placeholders are `subtle` at full opacity, scrollbars are `border-hover` on transparent, and focus is the 2px `focus` outline.

### Known exceptions
- Refreshing tables are dimmed to 0.62 opacity (`.is-stale`, 150ms fade) while a refetch is in flight. This is pre-existing and transient, and it drops secondary text below 4.5:1 (about 2.6–3.9:1) during the refresh. It is not a pattern to reuse for resting states.
- Threshold count panels and the dashboard list's mini previews use coloured 3–4px top borders on rounded cards. This is incumbent and limited to threshold colour; no other card type carries an accent border.
- Signal amber (`brand-light`) appears only in the Lighthouse brand mark. `src/app/icon.svg` is a static file that cannot read theme roles, so it repeats the brand values as literal colours with its own `prefers-color-scheme: dark` rule.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** take every colour from a `src/app/theme.css` role and define any new role once for light and once for dark.
- **Do** keep text roles at 4.5:1 or better on their surfaces in both themes, placeholders included.
- **Do** use solid Ocean blue (`primary`) only for the primary action, Run, the current rail tile, the selected tab underline and focus.
- **Do** show a selected filter or quick view as the soft blue pill (`primary-soft` with `accent` text).
- **Do** keep a text label beside every severity and status colour so meaning never rests on hue alone; add the shape-coded mark outside dense alert rows.
- **Do** keep resting panels flat (12px radius, 1px `border`, no shadow) and reserve navy-tinted shadows for floating objects.
- **Do** use Geist Mono for CEL, exact timestamps, source sub-lines and counts, with tabular figures for numbers; show times relative to now in Geist, with the exact time in the tooltip.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use black primary buttons or a black-and-white or grayscale palette; the user superseded them with Ocean.
- **Don't** adopt the Ocean prototype's structure: no 228px labelled sidebar, no Mulish or IBM Plex Mono, no halo on firing dots, no Ocean spacing or size changes.
- **Don't** write raw hex or rgba in feature styles.
- **Don't** add coloured accent borders to cards other than threshold count panels.
- **Don't** add eyebrow or kicker labels above page titles; the header is crumb row, title and subtitle.
- **Don't** show a standing Live indicator or revision numbers on the alert, preset or dashboard screens; the header reports update status only when something is wrong.
- **Don't** use signal amber (`brand-light`) outside the brand mark; warnings keep `amber` and `amber-mark`.
